# Budget years, party proposals and decisions

`public/data/gold/marts/budget-context.json` is the small navigation model behind the budget report. It covers the twelve annual central-government budgets for 2015–2026 (parliamentary sessions 2014/15–2025/26). It is built by `scripts/build-budget-context.py` from the checked-in FiU1 coverage, corrected budget frames, decision points, citations, reservations and votes. Every year links to its official FiU1 document. It is **not** a history of every supplementary budget, every appropriation decision or every Swedish budget ever adopted.

The government column represents the **submitted collective proposal**. It must not be copied into a separate M, KD, L, SD, S or MP party column. The 2023–2026 proposals came from the M/KD/L government under its SD budget agreement; missing separate columns for these parties are therefore not zeros. The 2020 and 2021 proposals from the S/MP government were agreed with C and L. [Riksdag: 2020 agreement](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/H701FIU2), [Riksdag: 2021 agreement](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/H801AU1), [Government: Tidö cooperation](https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/05/regeringen-och-sverigedemokraterna-presenterar-bokslut-over-tidoavtalet/).

For budget years **2015, 2019 and 2022**, the submitted government proposal was not the adopted frame. The adopted alternatives were the Alliance (M/C/L/KD), M/KD, and M/SD/KD respectively. Therefore a chart of the government's proposal for those years cannot be titled “the adopted budget.” This exception list is supported by the [government's review of the budget process](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/c3c8d6e9fa074489a0424e84d87e04f9/budgetprocessen-i-det-finanspolitiska-ramverket-sou-202393.pdf) and the linked FiU1 decisions.

The site has complete numeric 27-area government frames for 2017, 2020 and 2023–2026; 2018 and 2019 have partial extracted tables, which the report deliberately withholds from charts pending column validation. The 2015, 2016, 2021 and 2022 comparison documents are linked, but their numeric tables were not imported: the locally saved HTML is incomplete or uses another table layout. These are real coverage gaps, not zero proposals. The original source is available through each year's FiU1 link. A future repair should fetch the relevant official tables afresh, parse every area and proposal column, and reconcile totals against FiU1 before adding numbers.

The exact imported chain currently exists for two years: **FiU1 budget frame point 2 → vote ID → eight party positions**, for budget years 2025 and 2026. The site also lists explicitly cited propositions/motions and reservations at that point. A citation does not mean a party endorsed the document. Only one candidate speech was retrieved for the 2025 point and none for the 2026 point; that similarity is a discovery link, not an exact speech-to-motion relationship or evidence of why anyone voted No. Older FiU1 decisions are linked as documents, but historic member-level votes are not yet imported. [Riksdag: budget 2025](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/HC01FiU1), [Riksdag: budget 2026](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/HD01FiU1).

Party alignment is useful context, but “the right voted No because the left proposed it” is a hypothesis, not something a ballot proves. In the two imported recent government budget votes, M, KD, L and SD voted **Yes** to the committee proposal. To examine opposition behaviour fairly, a later analysis should distinguish government/committee proposals, rival whole-budget alternatives and individual motions, then compare only like-for-like votes across changes of government. It should quote a party's stated reason when making a claim about motive.

Regenerate and check this layer with `npm run data:build` and `npm run data:check`. The context validator checks all twelve years, original frame counts and every imported party position against its source fact row.
